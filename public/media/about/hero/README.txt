About page — the headline's inline photos

"We sit with [photo] your team, learn [photo] how you work, and build [photo] around it."
Three small photos sit inside the sentence; each slowly crossfades between 2–3 images.

What goes here
- 1-a.jpg, 1-b.jpg (, 1-c.jpg) : slot 1, after "We sit with" — sitting with a client team.
- 2-a.jpg, 2-b.jpg (, 2-c.jpg) : slot 2, after "learn" — observing / mapping how they work.
- 3-a.jpg, 3-b.jpg (, 3-c.jpg) : slot 3, after "and build" — the software in use.
Until a slot has files, it shows the owner's photos already on the site (see docs/PLACEHOLDERS.md).

Recommended sizes / formats
- Any image format / size (PNG, JPG, WebP, AVIF). The build crops nothing: frame the subject in the
  centre, landscape about 3:2 (the slot is ~1.45:1). Resized to 700 / 1400 px WebP + AVIF.
- Keep each source under ~3 MB (originals are deployed too).

Rules (all media): real photography only. No AI-generated images, no stock illustrations.
Record every third-party file in docs/CREDITS.md and tick it off in docs/PLACEHOLDERS.md.
