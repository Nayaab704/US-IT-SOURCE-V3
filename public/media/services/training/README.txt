Service: Technical Training

What goes here
- bg.* (bg.jpg, bg.png, bg.webp …) : ONE real full-bleed photograph that shows this service happening (e.g. a hands-on session, someone teaching at a screen or bench).
- Used full-screen as the chapter background in "Five ways we build around you" (detected automatically).

Also (optional) for the side visual:
- loop.webm + loop.mp4 : a short muted loop of a session (16:9, 1280-1920 wide, <= 4 MB each), or
- seq-01.jpg, seq-02.jpg ... : a slow photo sequence (16:9, 1600 wide, <= 250 KB each).

Recommended sizes / formats
- Any image format / size (PNG, JPG, WebP, AVIF). The build resizes and converts it (WebP + AVIF,
  1200 / 1600 / 2400 px, content-hashed URL). Originals are deployed too: keep the source under ~3 MB.
- 2400x1600 (3:2) or larger. Keep the left third and the top-right
  corner calm: the name and the short line sit there.

Rules (all media): real photography / real screens only. No AI-generated images, no stock
illustrations, no icons as decoration. Record every third-party file in
docs/CREDITS.md and tick it off in docs/PLACEHOLDERS.md.
